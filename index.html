Privacy Policy for The Greatest Generation

Effective date: August 25, 2026

The Greatest Generation is a single-player history trivia game. There is no game account, custom backend, or first-party analytics database.

Data stored locally

The game stores campaign progress, scores, achievements, settings, daily question state, and other gameplay state locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to the developer's servers. Removing the app removes this local data, subject to the behavior of your device backups.

Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple may receive and store leaderboard scores and achievements associated with your Game Center account. Apple's own privacy policy governs that service.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat may receive purchase history, product identifiers, entitlement status, and technical information needed to validate and restore the purchase. The game does not receive or store your payment card details.

Advertising and tracking

The game uses Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded revive advertisements. Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, the advertising SDK may collect device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction data, and technical information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

The game requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt when required. If permission or required advertising consent is not available, personalized advertising is not enabled and rewarded ads may be unavailable.

Sharing

Gameplay data is not sold by the developer. Apple Game Center, Apple StoreKit, RevenueCat, and Google Mobile Ads may process the information needed to provide their respective services under their own privacy policies.

Contact

For support, feedback, privacy questions, or requests concerning this policy, email zorgoball@gmail.com or visit The Greatest Generation support page.
